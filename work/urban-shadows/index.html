Urban environment / Temporal visualization · Penn MUSA 6950 · Academic project

A day in shadow.

Exploring changing shadow and sunlight

My role
Presentation and interpretation of a modeled shadow sequence
Tools
Spatial analysis · Raster visualization · Animation
Recorded outcome
Playable animation and manual frame exploration

The question

How does sunlight and shadow move across an urban surface over the course of the supplied sequence? An animation makes temporal change easier to follow than a single static map.

Read the sequence

Purple indicates modeled shadow, yellow indicates sunlight, and gray denotes NoData. The timestamp is embedded in each original frame. Use Play to follow the sequence or move the frame slider to inspect one moment at a time.

What the result means

The animation communicates modeled spatial patterns. It does not establish observed pedestrian exposure, thermal comfort, or a health effect. Those interpretations would require additional information about model inputs, people’s movement, and environmental conditions.

The ten frames come from the supplied shadow animation. The viewer preserves their order and timing; modeled surfaces are not measured pedestrian exposure.
